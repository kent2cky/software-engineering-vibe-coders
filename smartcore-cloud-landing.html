YOUR 12-WEEK LEARNING PATH

1. AWS Fundamentals & Core Services

Master the foundational AWS services: EC2, S3, RDS, VPC, and IAM. Understand regions, availability zones, and how to architect for high availability.

2. Identity, Access & Security

Deep dive into IAM policies, roles, and security best practices. Secure your infrastructure from day one with proper authentication and authorization.

3. Networking & Content Delivery

Design VPCs, configure subnets, implement load balancers, and optimize content delivery with CloudFront for global reach.

4. Databases & Data Storage

Learn relational (RDS) and NoSQL (DynamoDB) databases. Choose the right database for your use case and implement backup strategies.

5. Compute & Application Services

Master Lambda, API Gateway, and elastic compute solutions. Build serverless and traditional applications that scale automatically.

6. Monitoring, Logging & Troubleshooting

Implement CloudWatch, CloudTrail, and X-Ray for comprehensive monitoring. Debug and optimize your infrastructure in production.

7. Cost Optimization & Best Practices

Understand AWS pricing models, implement cost controls, and adopt architectural best practices recommended by AWS.

8. Infrastructure as Code & Automation

Master CloudFormation and Terraform to automate infrastructure deployment. Make your infrastructure reproducible and version-controlled.

9. Capstone Project & Certification Prep

Build a complete production-ready cloud solution. Prepare for AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate certification exam.
